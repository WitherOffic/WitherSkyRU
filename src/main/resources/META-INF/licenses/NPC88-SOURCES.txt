691 Russian literal translations with authored semantic colour anchors;

screenshots and these full-page revisions:

Per-record revision URLs are embedded in npc-expansion88.json and
npc-raw-repairs88.json. Full pages,conditions,blocks and all occurrences are
preserved in NPC_SOURCES_0.15.88.json and companion package records.

The immutable public corpus remains unchanged. Two old Sirius notification
translations were retained instead of adding redundant new keys. Archive
event dialogue and unpublished mechanics do not certify current live access.
